Object Summary

Anthropoid sarcophagus lid of Psamtik, Chief Physician and Overseer of Libyan Mercenaries (*Temehu*). Discovered in 1900 in a room-sized underground tomb at Saqqara, propped above its basin awaiting burial.

Material
Basalt (Greenish-Grey)
Dimensions / Mass
2.46 m • ~3.0 Tonnes
Chronology
26th Dynasty (c. 570–526 BCE)
Provenance
Saqqara (Unas Cemetery)
Epigraphic Corpus & Parallels

The inscriptions comprise protective spells from the Pyramid Texts (PT 630, 637, 638, 639) and Book of the Dead (BD 151), with speeches by the protective gods (*Nut*, the *Seven Shining Ones*, *Anubis*, *Isis*, *Nephthys*).

Archaeological Provenance

Tomb of Psamtik located at Saqqara near the Pyramid of Unas. A complementary stela belonging to Psamtik was discovered in the Serapeum of Saqqara.

Chronological Period

Dated to Year 26 of Pharaoh Amasis (Ahmose II, 570–526 BCE) during the Saite 26th Dynasty.
